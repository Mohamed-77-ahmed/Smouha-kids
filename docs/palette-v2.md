# Palette v2 "Koko"

Batch 6, request R8 (2026-10-18). Generated by `scripts/make-palette-sheet.ps1` from `app/src/main/res/values/colors.xml` and `values-night/colors.xml`; the swatch sheet is `docs/palette-v2.png`. Do not edit by hand: change the tokens, then run the script again.

## Where the colors come from

Sampled from the Koko pictures (`design/brand/koko.jpg`): rooster orange #F38E32, sunny yellow #FDC440, comb red #F15958, sky blue #C3E2EB (the icon's sky #D4EEF7), and the platform's #E86C2C, #48B7D3, #A7BC33, #DA558E.

- **Primary = the rooster orange family.** The picture's orange (#F38E32) is too light to carry white text (2.4:1) or to be read as text on cream, so primary is its deep tone #A84A0A (same hue family, 5.8:1 with white). The light orange lives in the containers (#FFE2C6), the confetti and the dark-mode primary (#FFB37A).
- **Secondary = the sunny yellow, with dark text.** #FDC440 itself is the secondary container: the selected chip, the PIN keys, the tonal buttons, always with dark brown text (#3B2800, 8.6:1). The `secondary` token is its dark ochre tone (#7A5700) for the few places Material draws secondary as text.
- **Tertiary = the sky blue.** Container #D4EEF7 (the icon's sky), accent #0B6A86 (the platform's #48B7D3, darkened for text).
- **Surfaces:** the page is a light sky tint (#F4FAFD); cards, the PIN sheet and dialogs are warm cream (#FFFCF6); the reward card stays white.
- **Header gradient:** light sky blue #BEE5F4 to soft yellow #FFDD80 (to bottom-right) through a cream center #FFF6E0. A straight sky-to-yellow blend turns grey-green in the middle; the cream stop keeps it sunny. Dark mode: deep sky #0B5577, dark navy #1C2E3D, deep gold #6B4A0E, white text.
- **Not brand colors:** the comb red stays out of the palette (red only means missed, overdue, off or a wrong PIN). The platform pink #DA558E is not used (it sits close to the purple range that CLAUDE.md 9 forbids).
- **Warnings:** amber lies between the brand's orange and yellow, so warning words alone would look like an orange section title. A warning row in parent settings is now an amber box with a 2dp border and a warning triangle, dark text inside (`bg_warning_row`, `ic_warning`). Status is never color alone: the words say what is wrong.
- **Dynamic color stays off**: the palette is always ours, in light and dark mode.
