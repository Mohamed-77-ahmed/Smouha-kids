Koko Dawa: handoff document for the hospital

App name on the phone: Koko Dawa (كوكو دوا), after its rooster mascot Koko; up to version 1.0.0 it was called Smouha Kids. Made for the patients of Smouha University Children's Hospital (مستشفى سموحة الجامعي للأطفال). Version 1.1.0 (versionCode 4), 2026-10-19; it updates 1.0.0 and keeps its data. This document describes the app; it is not an approval by the hospital, and the app does not claim one.

The app shows the hospital's logo, on a white tile, on its first screen, in the home header and on the About screen. Like the use of the name, this needs the hospital's approval (section 10).

1. Purpose

A medicine reminder for the hospital's patients (ages 0 to 18) and their families. A parent enters each medicine and its times; the phone reminds at those times and the child gets points and a short animation when a grown-up confirms the dose. It is a reminder tool only.

2. Features

  • Reminders per medicine: every day or chosen days, one or more times, optional start and end dates. Each reminder shows the patient's name and the medicine.
  • The app plays the phone's alarm sound for up to 30 seconds at each reminder, also when the phone is on silent or vibrate, with a "Stop sound" button. If a dose is not confirmed, up to 3 follow-up reminders (every 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes).
  • Several patients on one phone (for example siblings), each with their own points, streak and style by age group (child 0 to 12, teen 13 to 18, adult 19 and over).
  • A parent PIN (4 to 8 digits) confirms doses and protects adding, editing and deleting medicines. Points: +10 per dose, +5 on time, +20 for a complete day, bonuses for 3 to 100 days in a row; points are never taken away. A missed day ends the streak with a gentle message.
  • New in 1.1.0: the reward for a child or teen shows a celebration character with a short happy sound (media volume; a parent setting "Celebration sound", PIN-protected, on by default). When the last dose of a medicine with an end date is confirmed, a short video of a celebrating child says the recorded schedule is complete (never that the treatment is finished); it closes with a tap.
  • Egyptian Arabic and English. Works on small phones, large phones and tablets. An "About" screen with the disclaimer below.

4. Privacy

  • All data (patients, medicines, doses, points) stays in a database on the phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. No account, no analytics, no advertising.
  • No backup: Android's cloud backup is turned off for the app (allowBackup=false). Uninstalling the app or "Erase all data" deletes everything; data does not move to a new phone.
  • The parent PIN is stored only as a salted hash (PBKDF2), never as the PIN itself.

5. Safety limits

  • The app gives no medical advice, suggests no doses and checks no medicine names, doses or interactions. The parent types the medicine and the times; the doctor's instructions always come first.
  • A reminder can still be silenced by the phone: Do Not Disturb without alarms, alarm volume at 0, or a phone maker's battery saver. The app shows these states in parent settings, and the guides explain the battery settings.

6. Install and update

  • Families download Koko-Dawa-1.1.0.apk from a Google Drive link, allow installing from that source, pass the Play Protect warning (the app is not from Google Play) and install. The Arabic guides and the printable card describe each step.
  • Updates: a newer file, signed with the same key, is installed over the old one and keeps all data. A file signed with any other key cannot update the app.
  • File check: SHA256.txt next to this document holds the file's SHA-256. Signing certificate and its SHA-256:

7. Known limits

  • Android 9 or newer only. There is no iPhone version.
  • Battery settings matter: on some phones the maker's battery saver delays or stops reminders until the app is set to "unrestricted".
  • Data stays on one phone (no transfer, no backup). Changing the phone's clock can affect streaks. A child who knows the phone's screen lock can reset the parent PIN.
  • The Egyptian Arabic texts still need a review by a native speaker.

8. Devices actually tested

  • Emulator (Android 14, a Pixel phone profile): all automated tests, every screen in Arabic and English, screen sizes, large fonts, dark mode, and the signed release 1.1.0 itself (first use, a reminder, confirm with the PIN, reward, the course-complete video, and the update from 1.0.0 with every row of data kept).
  • One real phone: Samsung SM-A266B (Android 16, One UI 8.5). A pilot of 17 checks (silent, vibrate, Do Not Disturb, alarm volume, battery settings, reboot, locked screen, follow-ups, both languages, large font, PIN) was recorded as passed on a test build before the signed release; the results table does not record the phone model or the build. Neither signed release (1.0.0, 1.1.0) has been tested on a real phone yet.
  • Not tested: other phone brands (Xiaomi, Oppo, Realme, Vivo, Huawei), real phones with Android 9 to 15, tablets.
