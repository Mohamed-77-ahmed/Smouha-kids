Koko Dawa (for the patients of Smouha University Children's Hospital)
Version 1.1.0, versionCode 4, 2026-10-19

The same app that was called Smouha Kids, with a new name and look.
- The new name Koko Dawa (كوكو دوا) and the Koko rooster icon.
- New colors: light sky blue, sunny yellow and orange.
- The hospital's logo on the first screen, in the home header and on About.
- The reward for a child or teen shows a celebration character with a short happy sound. Parents can turn the sound off in parent settings ("Celebration sound", PIN-protected).
- When the last dose of a medicine with an end date is confirmed: a short video of a celebrating child and a message that the recorded schedule is complete.
- Everything else is unchanged: the alarm-sound reminders, the PIN, points and streaks.

Important:
- Installs over 1.0.0 and keeps all data (children, medicines, points, the PIN). Do not uninstall the old version first.
- File check: SHA256.txt. Signing certificate SHA-256, the same as 1.0.0:
  FF:0A:9A:1B:D4:09:D3:85:03:76:FF:A2:C8:3D:0F:27:E2:39:4D:7B:2A:B7:3E:D6:9E:16:14:3E:FF:8A:E8:7A
- A reminder tool only; it gives no medical advice.
